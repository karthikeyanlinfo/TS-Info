✳ Privacy

Your details, handled with care.

This page explains what happens to the information you send through the contact form on toxinsolutions.in.

What is collected

  • What you type into the form: name, email, optional phone, the service you’re interested in, optional goal, timeline and budget, and your message.
  • Basic context sent with the form: the page you submitted from, the referring page and any campaign tag in the link.

Why

Only to read and reply to your enquiry. Your details are not sold, rented or used for unrelated marketing.

Where it is stored

Submissions are stored in a private Google Sheet owned by Toxin Solutions (Google acts as the storage provider).

Sound & effects

The site’s sound is generated in your browser and is off until you switch it on. No cookies or tracking scripts are used for the visual effects.

How long

Enquiries are kept as long as needed to respond and follow up, and then deleted.

Your choices

You can ask for a copy of your details, or for them to be corrected or deleted, by replying to any email you receive from Toxin Solutions.
